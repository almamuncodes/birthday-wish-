HOW TO ADD YOUR SISTER'S REAL PHOTOS:
======================================

1. Copy your 4 favorite photos into this folder:
   e:\project 2\birthday gift\New folder\client\public\images\

2. Name them:
   - memory1.jpg  (e.g., photo of your first meeting / Facebook connection)
   - memory2.jpg  (e.g., photo of late night chats / fun sibling hangout)
   - memory3.jpg  (e.g., photo of her as a bride on her wedding day)
   - memory4.jpg  (e.g., photo of her in her new home or a recent picture)

3. Update the file paths in:
   src/data/birthdayData.js
   Change:
   image: "/images/memory1.svg" -> image: "/images/memory1.jpg"
   image: "/images/memory2.svg" -> image: "/images/memory2.jpg"
   image: "/images/memory3.svg" -> image: "/images/memory3.jpg"
   image: "/images/memory4.svg" -> image: "/images/memory4.jpg"

(You can also use .png or any URL link directly!)
